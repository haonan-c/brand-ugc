# Topic Strategy Card Contract

The machine-readable contract is `schemas/topic-candidates.schema.json`. The pending evidence pack remains the source of truth for dynamic count, audience IDs, quotas, demand keywords, and allowed source URLs.

## Evidence use

- `whyNow` explains why the topic matters in this bounded sample now.
- `evidenceSummary` states the concrete note/comment observations supporting the direction.
- `contentGap` is a cautious strategy inference, not a measured platform-wide gap.
- `sourceUrls` preserve complete `noteUrl` strings exactly.
- `demandKeywords` copy matching-audience seeds/terms exactly and are described only as autocomplete demand language.

## Creative execution

Every card must be directly usable by a creator: target scenario, differentiated angle, format, structure, title pattern, framework, hook, must-cover points, outline, and CTA. Alternatives must be meaningfully distinct, not punctuation variants.

## Scoring

Each dimension is an integer from 0 to 100:

- `demandFit` 25%
- `evidenceStrength` 20%
- `timingFit` 15%
- `differentiation` 15%
- `executability` 15%
- `riskSafety` 10%

The final score is the rounded weighted sum. Priority is derived mechanically: high at 80+, medium at 65–79, low below 65.

## Safety

Do not claim precise search volume, platform-wide trends, algorithm preferences, guaranteed virality, or factual/policy authority from social posts. When a card touches price, timing, eligibility, effects, policy, compliance, certification, benefits, or approval results:

- `riskNote` requires current official or authoritative verification;
- `doNotSay` explicitly forbids guarantees or deterministic outcomes.

Finalization rejects malformed cards before writing reports or marking a run completed.
